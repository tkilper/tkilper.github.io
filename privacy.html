Privacy Policy Last updated: March 28, 2026 168 Planner ("the App") is a personal time management tool. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used. Data Storage All data you enter, time logs, goals, energy ratings, sleep records, and settings, is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. Purchases The App offers an optional Pro upgrade (one-time purchase) processed through Apple's App Store via RevenueCat. Purchase and payment information is handled entirely by Apple. We receive only confirmation of whether an entitlement is active. We do not see or store your payment details. Advertising Free users may see banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers to serve contextually relevant ads. You can review Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Pro users see no ads. Analytics & Crash Reporting The App does not include any analytics or crash reporting SDKs. No usage data is sent to us. Health Data If you grant permission, the App reads sleep data from Apple Health to auto-fill your sleep log. This data is read-only, never leaves your device, and is not shared with any third party. Children's Privacy The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact Questions? Email us at: support168planner@gmail.com
